Status chips below are example labels so each state can be told apart. They are not calculated. No interval is approved. Lap readings and engine-hour readings are separate, and a missing reading is unknown, not healthy.

Six statuses

Not due

Neutral label. Not a healthy mark and not a ready mark.

Approaching

Inside the warning threshold. Threshold: — not set —.

Due

At the interval. Interval: TBD (admin rule required).

Overdue

Past the interval. Not calculated on this draft.

Failed inspection

Inspection did not pass. Distinct from a life interval.

Unknown

Missing history or readings. Hatched and dashed. Not a healthy status, and not green.
