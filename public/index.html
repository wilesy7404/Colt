Race team maintenance

Phase 1 data foundation for the 2004 Nissan 350Z. This page is a smoke check for the local API. It is not the race-day phone app.

Limitations

  • No service intervals or maintenance history are included. Empty readings stay unknown. Unknown is not healthy.
  • Offline sync, cross-device sync, and a native iPhone app are not implemented.
  • This screen does not certify that the car is safe to race.

Sign in
